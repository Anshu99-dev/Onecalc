Basic Calculator App – Privacy First, Easy to Use

About OneCalc

OneCalc is a lightweight, privacy-first basic calculator. It works offline, is fast, and has a simple design suitable for everyone.

Features:

  • Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division
  • Percentage (%) and Decimal support
  • Keyboard input support (numbers, + - * / . Enter, Backspace, C)
  • Backspace (←) and Clear (C) buttons
  • Local calculation history (stored in browser)
  • Privacy-first: no ads, no trackers, offline usage

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2025

OneCalc respects your privacy. This app does not collect personal information. All calculations and preferences are stored locally in your browser's storage and are never sent to any server or third party.

Optional features, if added in the future (like cloud sync), will request your consent before using external services.

Security: Data stored in your browser is as secure as your device and browser. Avoid using shared/public devices if privacy is a concern.

If you have questions or want your local data cleared, contact us at privacy@example.com.
